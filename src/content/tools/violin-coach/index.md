Honest practice feedback for solo beginner violinists. Upload a recording of your
playing — a phone video is fine — and get a detailed report on tuning, intonation,
rhythm and tone, plus a practice plan built from what it hears.

## Try it

Open the web version — no install, works on desktop and mobile:

- [vioanalyze.berkay.tools](https://vioanalyze.berkay.tools)

## How it works

- **Everything runs in your browser.** Your recording never leaves your device.
- **pYIN pitch tracking** and DSP find each note and measure how far it is from pitch,
  against A4 = 440 Hz (or 442, if that's how you tune).
- A Go analysis pipeline compiled to **WebAssembly** — the same engine as the
  command-line tool.
- Download the result as a self-contained HTML report, JSON, or a Markdown summary.

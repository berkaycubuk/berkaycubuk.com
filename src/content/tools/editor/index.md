A minimal, distraction-free text editor in your browser. Just a page to write on —
no accounts, no sync, no clutter.

## Try it

- [editor.berkay.tools](https://editor.berkay.tools)

## Features

- **Autosaves locally.** Your text stays in your browser between visits; nothing is sent
  anywhere.
- Open `.txt` and `.md` files, and export back to a file.
- Light and dark themes — follows your system until you pick one.
- Keyboard shortcuts: <kbd>Cmd/Ctrl+S</kbd> exports, <kbd>Cmd/Ctrl+O</kbd> opens, and
  <kbd>Tab</kbd> inserts a real tab.
